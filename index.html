Medical safety note

FlowWell supports tracking and planning, but it is not medical advice. Predictions are estimates and should not be used as contraception. Seek urgent care for severe pain, fainting, fever, unusual discharge, or bleeding that soaks pads very quickly.

Emergency & Health Resources

Quick help, practical steps, and trusted support.

Crisis protocol

  1. Move to a safe, private place if you can, and sit or lie down.
  2. Call campus health support or a trusted friend to stay with you.
  3. If pain is severe, bleeding is unusually heavy, or you feel faint, seek urgent medical care.
  4. Keep a short note of symptoms and medicines taken to help the nurse understand quickly.
